Strip Night Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Strip Night mobile game (the "Game") handles when you play it. The short version: we never ask for your real name or email, and your progress stays on your device. Data leaves your device only for the advertising and payment services described below, and, if you choose to race online, for your racer name and your best recorded runs.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (cash, cars, upgrades, settings and story choices) is saved locally on your device. We do not receive a copy of it. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, that progress is deleted and we cannot recover it.

2. Information we collect

We do not sell personal information, and the Game has no sign-up, no chat and no email or password login. The only information the Game itself sends anywhere is for online races, described in section 3.

3. Online races

Online races are optional. They let other players race a recording of your best run, and let you race theirs. They only start once you choose a racer name in the Online tab. When they are on:

  • Anonymous account: the Game creates an anonymous account with Google Firebase Authentication. It is a random ID with no link to your name, email or Google account.
  • Racer name: the name you choose (3 to 16 letters or numbers). Other players see it. Please do not use your real name.
  • Your best run in each class: your times and speeds, the car and its cosmetic setup (paint, wheels, body kit), and a recording of the run (position, speed, rpm and gear over time). Other players can download it to race against and see it on leaderboards.

This data is stored by Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) on Google's servers and is kept until you ask us to delete it. Google's processing is covered by Firebase's privacy information. To delete your online data, email us with your racer name and we will remove your ghosts and racer name within 30 days. Uninstalling the Game removes the anonymous ID from your device but does not delete what was already uploaded.

4. Advertising

The Game shows ads using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system, app activity and approximate location. This is governed by Google's policies: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads). You can also remove ads inside the Game with the "Remove ads" purchase, where offered.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. Google Play tells the Game whether a purchase succeeded so it can unlock the item. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.

6. Permissions

  • Internet: used to load ads, to process purchases and for online races.
  • Advertising ID: used by the advertising service described above.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help get it removed from any service we control.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the third-party services above (Google AdMob, Google Play and Google Firebase) as needed to run ads, purchases and online races. Your racer name and best runs are visible to other players when you race online.

9. Your choices

  • Delete all local game data by uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage in Android settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android settings.
  • Turn off vibration and sound in the Game's Settings.
  • Race offline only by not setting a racer name, or ask us to delete your online data (section 3).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the updated policy.
