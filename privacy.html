Strip Night Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information the Strip Night mobile game (the "Game") handles when you play it. The short version: the Game has no accounts, we do not ask for your name or email, and your progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what the advertising and payment services described below collect.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (cash, cars, upgrades, settings and story choices) is saved locally on your device. We do not receive a copy of it. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, that progress is deleted and we cannot recover it.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal information on our own servers. The Game has no sign-up, no chat and no user-generated content.

3. Advertising

The Game shows ads using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system, app activity and approximate location. This is governed by Google's policies: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads). You can also remove ads inside the Game with the "Remove ads" purchase, where offered.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. Google Play tells the Game whether a purchase succeeded so it can unlock the item. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: used to load ads and to process purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the advertising service described above.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help get it removed from any service we control.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the third-party services above (Google AdMob and Google Play) as needed to run ads and purchases.

8. Your choices

  • Delete all local game data by uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage in Android settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android settings.
  • Turn off vibration and sound in the Game's Settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the updated policy.
