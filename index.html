Strip Night

Quarter-mile drag racing. 1,320 feet, two lanes, one green light. Beat each rival, spend the prize money in the garage, and chase a faster time slip.

  1. Hold Go and Gas. Go holds the car on the line. Rev to the ideal marker, then tap Gas to keep the needle on it.
  2. Release Go the instant the green light comes on. Let go early and it is a red light. Keep Gas down off the line and ease off if the grip bar goes red.
  3. Tap Shift (same button) when the lights hit red. N2O gives one two-second push.
